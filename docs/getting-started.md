# Getting started

These five pages orient a reader before working through the five [stages](glossary.md#stage). Read them once, in the order listed, before Stage 1. None of them is a stage of the framework. Together they answer what the method is, what you need, the one example that threads through every stage, who decides what, and what your own [agentic platform](glossary.md#agentic-platform) must supply.

## Pages in this section

- [Pipeline overview](pipeline-overview.md): the framework's own shape, all five stages on one page, with a figure.
- [How to use this guide](how-to-use-this-guide.md): what you need, how to get the files, how pages are laid out, and how to read prompts and scripts.
- [The running example](running-example.md): the one small, fictional program every stage uses, so its prompts, data, and scripts stay consistent.
- [Human roles, gates and batching](human-roles-gates-and-batching.md): who decides what, where work pauses, and how work is sized.
- [Platform requirements](platform-requirements.md): what your own agentic platform must offer, with a readiness checklist.
