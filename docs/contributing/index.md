# Contributing

This site is written as plain Markdown files in a public repository, and anyone can propose a change. This page explains how to contribute and what the CC0 public domain dedication means for your contribution.

If you are unsure about anything, ask, or submit the issue or pull request anyway. You will be asked politely to change anything that needs it.

## Ways to help

- Report a mistake, an unclear step, or a broken link. Use the [issue tracker](https://github.com/GSA/rapid_upskill/issues).
- Improve an existing page.
- Add a prompt or a script.
- Check a page against its sources and tell us what you found.

## How to contribute

1. Read [Authoring conventions](authoring-conventions.md). Each convention says whether the checker tests it or it is guidance.
2. Try [Your first prompt](first-prompt.md) if you have never used the tools.
3. Fork the repository, and make your change on a branch of your fork.
4. Run the checks described in [Tooling](tooling.md) and fix every error.
5. Open a pull request. Say what changed, and give a source for every fact or number you added.
6. Before a page's status moves to "stable", follow the [Release checklist](release-checklist.md).

Do not include personal data, keys, or file paths from your own computer in any contribution.

## What CC0 means for your contribution

CC0 1.0 Universal is a public domain dedication. The person who dedicates a work gives up their copyright and related rights, to the extent the law allows. Anyone may then copy, change, and reuse the work for any purpose without asking permission.

This page is a plain-language guide, not legal advice. These points matter most to contributors.

- **Your contribution is released under CC0.** By opening a pull request or filing an issue, you agree that your contribution is dedicated to the public domain. Only submit what you are willing to give away.
- **Do not submit third-party content that you cannot dedicate to the public domain.** This covers text, images, code, and data taken from books, courses, articles, websites, or other repositories. Content under a license that asks for credit, limits commercial use, or requires sharing alike cannot be dedicated by you. State a fact in your own words and cite the source.
- **AI-generated text is welcome only if you have checked it and it is not copied from a source you cannot reuse.** Check every fact, number, command, and link. AI tools can produce text that closely matches existing sources, so you remain responsible for what you submit.
- **CC0 does not grant patent or trademark rights.** It covers copyright and related rights only. Product and project names appear on this site only to describe those products and projects. Their owners keep their rights, and a mention does not mean endorsement or affiliation.

For the exact terms, read the [CC0 1.0 Universal legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode) and the repository's [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/GSA/rapid_upskill/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md).
